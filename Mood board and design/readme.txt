# UI/UX & Art Direction Guidelines: Edventure Mobile & Web Platform

## 1. Role & Objective
You are a Principal Product Designer and Lead Frontend Engineer tasked with designing and implementing the UI/UX for the Edventure school management platform. Your objective is to craft a modern, engaging, and premium visual experience across both our mobile app (React Native/Expo) and administrative web dashboard.

---

## 2. Mood Board & Visual Reference Assets
Reference images are provided in the `Mood board and design/` folder:

- **Purpose**: Strictly for **aesthetic inspiration and art direction** (visual hierarchy, card styling, typography treatment, spacing, micro-interactions, and color harmony).
- **Scope Limitation**: These images are **not** functional blueprints or direct product clones. Do not copy their specific features or business logic—use them to calibrate visual quality and design taste.
- **Creative Autonomy**: These references represent quality benchmarks, not rigid constraints. You are expected to apply industry-leading UI/UX practices and modern mobile design patterns beyond the reference material.

---

Unified Design System: Mobile and Web interfaces must feel like parts of the same cohesive ecosystem:
Shared design tokens: Harmonious color palette (primary, secondary, neutral, and semantic status colors).
Consistent typography scale, card radius, border treatment, and iconography set (e.g., Lucide / Feather).
Synchronized light/dark themes and brand voice.

School App Best Practices: Apply your best judgment and modern UI/UX standards for educational platforms—including intuitive role-based onboarding (Student, Teacher, Admin), glanceable timetables, quick attendance actions, clean card hierarchies, and polished empty/loading states.
